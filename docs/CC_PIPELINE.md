# CC Forge — Photo/Description to Sims 4 CC\n\nCC Forge starts from a normal clothing photo, a text description, or both. A source .package is not required from the user.\n\n## Pipeline\n1. Source: photo and/or description.\n2. Maxis Match design concept.\n3. Clothing specification: garment type, silhouette, construction details, colors and materials.\n4. Texture generation.\n5. Sims 4 CAS mesh generation/adaptation.\n6. CASP configuration.\n7. DBPF package assembly.\n8. Validation and download.\n\n## Sims 4 resources\n- GEOM 0x015A1849 — CAS part geometry.\n- CASP 0x034AEECB — CAS Part data.\n- DDS 0x00B2D882, RLE2 0x3453CF95, RLES 0xBA856C78 — texture resources.\n\n## Product rule\nNever present an image preview as a playable .package. The final download must only be offered after a real DBPF package has been assembled and validated.
